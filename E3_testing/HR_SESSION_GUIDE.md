# HR feedback session (15–20 min per person)   STATUS: TEMPLATE, no sessions held
Who: 3 to 5 people who work in HR or recruiting. Not team members, not Claude/ChatGPT.
Before: open hr_review_prototype.html with a case file (real CV cases are private: hand over in person or by a private channel; never upload).
1. Say: "The tool only gives advice. You decide." Do not explain the screen.
2. Give them 2 cases. Ask them to review each and save a decision. Note time, hesitation and questions.
3. Ask: what does "Not stated (unknown, not negative)" mean? (check they do not read it as "lacks")
4. Ask: would you override the suggestion on either case? Why?
5. Fill HR_feedback_sheet_TEMPLATE.csv (one row per case). Record verbatim comments, not summaries.
Report: counts and quotes only, no CV text. Participants stay anonymous (P1, P2, ...).
